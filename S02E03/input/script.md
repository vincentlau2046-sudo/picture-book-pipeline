# S02E03 - 虚荣者的星球

**合集：** 琪琪故事花园-小王子
**序列号：** S02E03
**风格：** 蜡笔儿童手绘

---

## 中文旁白

小王子飞呀飞，来到了一个特别亮的星球。
一个人穿着闪亮的衣服，戴着一顶大帽子。
"你好呀！你看我帅不帅？"
那个人一直问。
小王子说："帅呀。"
那个人开心极了。"那你鼓鼓掌嘛！"
小王子拍了拍手。
那个人笑得更开心了。"再多拍一点！再多拍一点！"
琪琪也试了试那顶大帽子。
可是……帽子里只有自己。
"我鼓掌，也只有自己听。"
琪琪嘟了嘟嘴。
"一个人夸自己，好没意思呀。"
小王子想了想。
"我有一朵玫瑰。我每天夸她，她也夸我。"
"那才开心呢。"
那个人还在喊——"快夸我！快夸我！"
可是没有人理他。
琪琪小声说——
"一直只看自己，会不会很孤单呀？"
小王子点点头。
"是呀，看见别人，别人才会看见你。"
那个人还在照镜子。
小王子和琪琪悄悄地飞走了。
小朋友们，你最喜欢夸谁呀？
快去夸夸你的好朋友吧~

## 英文旁白

The Little Prince flew and flew, and arrived at a very bright planet.
A man wore shiny clothes and a big hat.
"Hello! Am I handsome?"
The man kept asking.
The Little Prince said, "Yes, you are."
The man was so happy. "Then clap for me!"
The Little Prince clapped his hands.
The man laughed even more. "Clap more! Clap more!"
Qiqi tried on that big hat too.
But... the hat only had herself inside.
"When I clap, only I can hear it."
Qiqi pouted.
"Praising yourself alone is so boring."
The Little Prince thought.
"I have a rose. I praise her every day, and she praises me too."
"That's what's fun."
The man kept shouting — "Praise me! Praise me!"
But nobody paid attention to him.
Qiqi whispered —
"Only looking at yourself... wouldn't that be lonely?"
The Little Prince nodded.
"Seeing others is how others see you."
The man was still looking in the mirror.
The Little Prince and Qiqi quietly flew away.
Kids, who do you like to praise?
Go praise your best friend~
