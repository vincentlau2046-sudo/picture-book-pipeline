# S02E02 - 小王子遇见奇怪的星球

**合集：** 琪琪故事花园-小王子
**序列号：** S02E02
**风格：** 蜡笔儿童手绘

---

## 中文旁白

小王子回过头，最后看了一眼他的小星球。然后，他在宇宙里飞呀飞。
第一站，他来到了一个全是房子的星球。
一个人坐在高高的椅子上，戴着金色的帽子。
"我是这里的国王！所有的人都得听我的命令！"
小王子问："那你可以命令太阳落山吗？"
国王想了想……"嗯……太阳不太听话。"
"那我命令你……问我可不可以落山！"
小王子觉得好奇怪——"当了国王，也不能让太阳听话呀。"
琪琪小声说——"当国王好威风呀，可是没有朋友一起玩，会不会很孤单呢？"
第二站，是一个很亮的星球。
一个人穿着闪亮的衣服，不停地照镜子。
"我帅不帅？我帅不帅！"
小王子说："帅呀。"
那个人开心了一秒钟，又问——"那你再说说，我是不是最帅的？"
小王子走了，那个人还在追着喊——"等等！我还没听够！"
琪琪嘟了嘟嘴——"一直让人夸，还不如画一幅画……"
她用手指在星星上画画，画出一朵歪歪扭扭的小花，自己看着笑了。
"妈妈说我画得真用心，那才开心呢！"
小王子坐在自己的小星球上，飞过星空，托着腮帮子发呆。
第三站，是一个数星星的星球。
一个人坐在那里，数啊数啊。"五亿一百万……五亿一百万零一……"
"你在做什么呀？"小王子问。
"数星星！它们都是我的！"
"你数它们做什么呢？"
"为了拥有更多呀！"
小王子想了想——我有一朵玫瑰，我每天给她浇水，给她盖玻璃罩，她开花的时候好漂亮！那才是真正的喜欢呀。
光数着数字，又不能抱抱星星，有什么意思呢？
琪琪什么也没说，只是轻轻地抱了抱身边的小星星。
小王子抬头看了看远方的星空。
他想——玫瑰，你还好吗？
我在别的星球上，学会了很多。
可是，最想回去的地方……还是你的身边呀。
小王子转过身，朝回家的方向，迈出了一步。

## 英文旁白

The Little Prince looked back one last time at his tiny planet. Then, he flew through the stars.
First, he arrived at a planet full of houses.
A man sat on a tall chair, wearing a golden hat.
"I am the King! Everyone must obey my commands!"
The Little Prince asked, "Then can you command the sun to set?"
The King thought for a moment... "Hmm... the sun doesn't listen very well."
"Then I command you... to ask me if you may set!"
The Little Prince thought this was very strange. "Even a King can't make the sun obey."
Qiqi whispered — "Being a King must be so powerful! But with no friends to play with... wouldn't it be lonely?"
Next, he came to a very bright planet.
A man wore shiny clothes and kept looking in the mirror.
"Am I handsome? Am I handsome?"
The Little Prince said, "Yes, you are."
The man was happy for one second, then asked — "Then tell me, am I the most handsome?"
The Little Prince walked away, and the man chased after him — "Wait! I haven't heard enough!"
Qiqi pouted — "Always wanting praise... I'd rather draw a picture..."
She used her finger to draw on a star, making a wobbly little flower, and smiled at it.
"Mom says 'You really put your heart into it.' That's what makes me happy!"
The Little Prince sat on his tiny planet, drifting through the stars, resting his chin on his hand.
The third planet had a man counting stars.
He sat there, counting and counting. "Five hundred and one million... five hundred and one million and one..."
"What are you doing?" the Little Prince asked.
"Counting stars! They're all mine!"
"What do you count them for?"
"To own more, of course!"
The Little Prince thought — I have a rose. I water her every day and cover her with a glass dome. When she blooms, she's so beautiful! That's what truly loving something means.
Just counting numbers — you can't even hug a star. What's the point?
Qiqi said nothing. She just gently hugged the little star beside her.
The Little Prince looked up at the distant stars.
He thought — Rose, are you okay?
I've learned so much on other planets.
But the place I want to return to most... is right by your side.
The Little Prince turned around, and took one step toward home.
